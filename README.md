# 📈 投资心路：稳健底色下的全球视野 (Investment Philosophy: Stability & Vision)

### 🛡️ 我的背景与底线 / Background & Bottom Line
- **中文**: 我出身平凡，深知财富积累的不易，因此我的投资风格偏向保守。过去，我的证券账户中只有美债，因为对我而言，“保本”是第一准则。
- **English**: Coming from a humble background, I deeply value the hard-earned nature of wealth, which makes my investment style naturally conservative. In the past, my portfolio consisted solely of US Treasuries, as "capital preservation" was my primary rule.

### 🚀 认知的跃迁 / Cognitive Shift
- **中文**: 最近我深刻感受到，全球科技的进步与生态高度集中。我认为投资纳斯达克指数（Nasdaq）本质上是投资人类科技的未来。
- **English**: Recently, I’ve realized that global technological advancement and ecosystem growth are highly concentrated. I believe that investing in the Nasdaq index is, in essence, investing in the future of human technology.

### ⚖️ 杠铃策略 / The Barbell Strategy
- **中文**: 虽然意识到机遇，但我依然尊重风险。我制定了“九一原则”：
    - **90% 仓位**：继续加仓美债，作为生活的坚实后盾。
    - **10% 仓位**：定投纳指，尝试捕捉全球顶尖科技的成长红利。
- **English**: Despite seeing the opportunities, I still respect the risks. I have established a "90/10 Principle":
    - **90% Allocation**: Continue increasing US Treasury holdings as a solid financial backbone.
    - **10% Allocation**: Invest in the Nasdaq index to capture the growth dividends of world-class technology.

---

### 📝 寄语 / Reflection
> “投资不仅是金钱的游戏，更是认知的变现。在通往 37 岁的路上，我希望在守护安全的同时，也能拥抱星辰大海。”
> "Investing is not just a game of money, but the monetization of one's insights. On my path to 37, I aim to protect my security while embracing the vast future of technology."
